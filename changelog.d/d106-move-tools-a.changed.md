The inbox tools (`inbox_add`, `inbox_list`, `inbox_claim`, `inbox_release`, `inbox_set_status`) and the standing-order tools (`order_add`, `order_list`, `order_remove`) now live in the operations registry of the core, and the MCP server builds them from it; their arguments, answers and refusals are the ones they always had, pinned by a golden test, and the cost of `tools/list` is unchanged. / As ferramentas da caixa de entrada (`inbox_add`, `inbox_list`, `inbox_claim`, `inbox_release`, `inbox_set_status`) e das ordens permanentes (`order_add`, `order_list`, `order_remove`) agora vivem no registro de operações do núcleo, e o servidor MCP as monta dele; argumentos, respostas e recusas são os de sempre, fixados por um teste dourado, e o custo de `tools/list` não mudou.
