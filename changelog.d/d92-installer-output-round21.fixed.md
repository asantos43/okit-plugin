`status` no longer exits 1 when a field of `quota/<client>.json` holds a value of the wrong type, in Claude Code, Codex and opencode: a `recordedAt` that is not text that is a date (for example an object with no `toString`) is no date, so the reading shows without its age, and the fields of the `windows` of an automatic reading are kept only when they are numbers or dates (one shared reader, `src/quota-fields.js`). A test runs a corpus of odd values over every field and client in both languages.
